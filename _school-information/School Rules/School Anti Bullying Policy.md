<h2>School Anti-Bullying Policy</h2>
<p><strong><u>Our Commitment</u></strong>
</p>
<p>At Corporation Primary School, we believe every child deserves to feel
safe, valued, competent and empowered in their learning journey. We are
committed to creating a caring and enabling school environment where all
students can flourish and develop positive relationships with their peers.</p>
<p>Our approach focuses on teaching respect, compassion, and empathy while
addressing any hurtful behaviour that may arise. We work together as a
school community – staff, students, and parents – to ensure everyone feels
they belong.</p>
<p><strong><u>What are hurtful behaviours and bullying?</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Hurtful behaviours are unkind and hurtful acts such as once-off insensitive
comments, actions and physical fights, while bullying involves persistent
and intentional hurtful acts.</p>
<p>Hurtful behaviours and bullying can take various forms, including verbal,
social, physical and cyber:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Verbal: Name-calling, spreading rumours, or making threats</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Social exclusion: Deliberately leaving someone out, or encouraging others
to ignore someone</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Physical actions: Pushing, hitting, or damaging someone's belongings</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cyber bullying: Using online technology like phones, computers, or social
media to hurt or embarrass others on purpose and repeatedly</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><u>The school’s firm stance against hurtful behaviours</u></strong>
</p>
<p><strong>The school takes a firm stance against all forms of hurtful behaviours</strong>,
from once-off, insensitive comments to persistent intentional acts of bullying
and physical violence. This stance guides our school in managing different
forms of hurtful behaviours.</p>
<p>We recognise that children may have disagreements or say things they do
not mean. We help students understand the difference between occasional
conflicts (which are normal) and persistent bullying behaviour, addressing
each situation with care and appropriate support.</p>
<p>&nbsp;<strong><u>How we prevent incidents</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Taking a firm stance against</strong> hurtful behaviours and bullying</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Fostering a caring and enabling school environment</strong>, with
positive teacher-student relationships and strong peer support structure
with trained student leaders</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Conducting Character &amp; Citizenship Education (CCE) lessons</strong> on
empathy, conflict management, resilience and cyber wellness</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Having regular communications</strong> with staff and students</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong><u>How we respond</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Physical and psychological safety</strong> measures for all students
involved</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Investigation</strong> and timely updates, subject to case complexity</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Consequences, </strong>including disciplinary actions, conduct
grade adjustments, learning prosocial skills and reconciliation of relationships</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Support plans</strong> for affected students, including victims
and offender</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Restorative support</strong> including school counselling, external
referrals and community resources</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Partnership with parents</strong> throughout the process, including
informing parents at the earliest opportunity</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><u>How parents can support</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Talk regularly</strong> with your child about school experiences
and friendships</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Teach respectful behaviour</strong> and reinforce healthy digital
habits at home</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Monitor</strong> your child’s online and offline interactions and
activities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Explain</strong> why certain behaviours are inappropriate using
everyday experiences</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Model positive behaviour</strong> in your own interactions, such
as being calm and respectful in communications</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Share concerns promptly</strong> with your child’s Form Teacher
or Year Head</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Work collaboratively</strong> with school on agreed interventions</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We will review this policy regularly to make sure it continues to serve
our school community well. Together, we shape our students’ character,
strengthen relationships, and build a caring society.</p>
